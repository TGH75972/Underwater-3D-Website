UNDERWATER EXPLORATION

A cinematic underwater world built using Three.js featuring a fully textured submarine, coral reefs, rocks, bubbles, and atmospheric deep ocean lighting.

Advanced Submarine

The submarine is modeled entirely with geometries including fins, propellers, windows, pipes, and realistic movement underwater.

Coral Reef Ecosystem

Colorful coral reefs and scattered rocks simulate a dense underwater biome at the bottom of the ocean floor.

The Abyss

Deep blue fog, floating particles, and volumetric lighting recreate the mysterious environment of the deep sea :O
